# Dataset

## Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention

- **Records:** 12,330 shopping sessions.
- **Inputs:** 17 features.
- **Target:** `Revenue` — purchase or no purchase.
- **File:** [`raw/online_shoppers_intention.csv`](raw/online_shoppers_intention.csv), included without intentional modifications.
